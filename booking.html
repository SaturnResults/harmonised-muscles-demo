Booking FAQ

Do I pay when I book?

No. There's no account and no card needed to book. You pay at the clinic after your session, by card or cash.

Which treatment should I book?

For a specific injury or tight, knotted muscles, Deep Tissue is a good start. For long-standing pain, sciatica-type symptoms or headaches, Myofascial Release often helps. If you're not sure, book either one. Eoin talks through your symptoms first and adjusts the session to suit.

Can I move or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Call or text Eoin on 07944 493172 at least 24 hours before your appointment and he'll move or cancel it for free.

How do packages work?

Choose a 3, 6 or 10 session package and book your first session now. Sessions can be used for deep tissue or myofascial release, and packages are valid for 4, 8 or 12 months. They also make a great gift.

Can I book a longer session?

Yes. Sessions are usually 60 minutes, and 90 or 120-minute sessions are available on request. Add a note in the last step of your booking and Eoin will arrange it.

Is deep tissue massage painful?

It can feel more intense than a relaxing massage, but it should never be unbearable. Eoin always works within your pain threshold and adjusts the pressure as he goes.

Where is the clinic?

135 Dulwich Road, London SE24 0NG, in Herne Hill. Herne Hill station is a two-minute walk, with buses and free and paid parking nearby. The clinic is wheelchair accessible.
