Expert Sports Injury and Myofascial Therapy

Eoin O'Connell is a BTEC Level 6 Sports Injury Massage Therapist specialising in myofascial release. A former Gaelic football and hurling player who reached All-Ireland finals, he retrained after a hamstring injury that kept coming back.

Today he helps men and women with recurring injuries and chronic pain recover, move freely and get back to the activities they love, with a focused treatment and a tailor-made plan to follow at home.
